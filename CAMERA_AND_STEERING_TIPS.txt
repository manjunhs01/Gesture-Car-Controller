GESTURE CAR — CAMERA / LOW-LAG PROFILE

Changes in this build:
- Requests 640x480 at 30 FPS (often more reliable than requesting 60 FPS from a built-in webcam).
- Uses 480px inference width to reduce MediaPipe processing delay.
- Slightly more tolerant hand detection/tracking confidence thresholds.
- If hands are lost for 0.2 seconds, output returns to neutral to avoid a stuck steering command.

SETUP FOR THE CAMERA
1. Put the laptop on a stable surface and use bright, even light facing your hand.
2. Keep your entire hand and wrist visible in the camera preview. Do not put the hand extremely close to the lens; start about 50–80 cm away and adjust until the hand is clearly visible.
3. Avoid backlighting, dark rooms, motion blur, and other apps using the webcam.
4. Run run.bat. Keep your hand inside the camera view and perform calibration with a steady neutral pose.
5. For browser games, click the game window after the controller starts so keyboard input goes to the game.

If delay remains, close video-call/browser camera tabs and check the app's displayed inference FPS/latency. This software cannot guarantee a particular FPS on every webcam/PC. Test in a game/simulator only, never a real vehicle.
